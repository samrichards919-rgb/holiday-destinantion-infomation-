Holiday Planning Challenge • Family of 4 (2 Adults, 2 Children) • 7 Nights Overseas • $15,000 AUD Total Budget (70% Rule: Flights + Hotels ≤ $10,500)
Step 4 Evaluation Tool

Destination Rating Guide (Score Out of 30)

Rate your destination from 1 to 5 in each of the 6 categories below. Always back up your score with a "Score + Proof" fact from the information report!

Category Guiding Questions 1 Point (Poor Fit) 3 Points (Okay Fit) 5 Points (Ideal Fit)
1. Travel Time & Geography How far is it from Adelaide? Are there direct flights, or will long layovers eat into our 7-night holiday? Very far (18+ hrs), multiple layovers, loses 2+ days to flying. Medium distance (9–14 hrs) with 1 easy stopover. Close to Adelaide (under 8 hrs) or a direct flight available.
2. Climate & Season What is the weather like in our chosen window (June/July or Sept/Oct)? Will rain or extreme heat/cold ruin plans? Extreme weather (monsoon rains, extreme heat/cold) in our window. Mixed weather; some rain or slightly too hot/cold. Ideal, comfortable weather for sightseeing and outdoor activities.
3. Culture & Food How easy is it to communicate, eat, and get around? Are there exciting cultural experiences for kids? Major language barrier and few familiar/kid-friendly foods. English spoken in tourist spots; good mix of local and familiar food. Very easy to communicate and navigate; exciting culture and food.
4. Affordability (Economy) How strong is the AUD there? Are daily costs cheap or expensive, and do flights + hotels stay under $10,500? Expensive currency; daily costs high; flights/hotels exceed $10,500. Moderate prices; fits $15,000 budget if we spend carefully. Great exchange rate; meals, transport, and tickets very affordable.
5. Attractions & Activities Are there at least three standout landmarks or activities that both adults and kids will enjoy? Few family-friendly attractions, or activities are repetitive. A few good attractions, though some suit adults or younger kids only. Packed with diverse, exciting landmarks and activities for all ages.
6. Family Suitability & Safety How safe and easy is it to travel with children? Are visas, health rules, and transport simple? Complex visas/health rules, or tricky/unsafe transport for kids. Safe in main tourist areas; requires basic planning for visas/transport. Extremely safe, clean, easy public transport, simple entry rules.
